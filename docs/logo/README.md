# Oxia Chaos logo

The logo retains the six-part Oxia pinwheel so it remains visibly part of the
Oxia family. One segment is displaced and colored orange to represent a bounded,
intentional disruption. The orange center and white bolt represent the fault
injection point.

## Colors

- Oxia blue: `#0B74F5`
- Chaos orange: `#FF5A36`
- Bolt: `#FFFFFF`

The SVG has a transparent background and is the source asset. Keep clear space
around the mark equal to at least half the diameter of its center circle. At very
small sizes, the orange displacement remains the primary distinction even if the
bolt is no longer visible.
